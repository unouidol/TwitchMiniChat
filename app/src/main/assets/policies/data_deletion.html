Data Deletion

Last updated: 2026-09-15

TwitchMiniChat has four ways to delete data from inside the app, and you can also ask for deletion by email. Only two of the in-app options contact the server. For each one, this page says what it removes from the phone and what it does, or does not do, on the server.

In the app, open Safety & Privacy, then Data & Account Control. "Reset local data" offers the first three options below; "Delete app account and all data" is a separate button. To remove a single account, use the X next to it in the account list.

Reset local data, keep accounts

  • On this phone: removes the app's settings and preferences, including alert settings, hidden users and Most Wanted selections, together with cached data, the local diagnostic record, and your acceptance of the terms, which you are asked for again. Your accounts, their sign-in with the TwitchMiniChat server, and the built-in browser's website data are kept, and so is the credential the server knows this phone by.
  • On the server: nothing. No request is sent. This phone stays registered for alerts, and the options below can still remove that registration later.

Erase everything on this device

  • On this phone: removes every account and its sign-in with the TwitchMiniChat server, the app's settings and preferences, hidden users, cached data, the local diagnostic record, your acceptance of the terms, and the built-in browser's website data, such as the cookies of the Pokémon Community Game pages. The browser data is cleared first; if that fails, nothing is erased and the app shows the error.
  • On the server: nothing. No request is sent. This phone stays registered for alerts, so alerts for the accounts that were on it can keep arriving. Erasing also removes the credential this phone would need to remove that registration later. If you want alerts to stop, choose the next option instead. If you have already erased, ask for the registration to be removed by email.

Erase everything and remove this device from the server

  • On the server, first: removes this phone's registration for alerts. Data belonging to your accounts, such as uploaded Pokédex lists and sign-in records, is kept, and your other devices are not affected.
  • On this phone, only after the server has confirmed: everything the previous option removes. If the server request fails, nothing on the phone is erased and the app shows the error.
  • Limit: the server identifies this phone by a credential stored in the app. "Erase everything on this device" removes it, and so did every reset in versions before 5.5.1. A registration this phone had before such an erase or reset is therefore not reached; ask for its removal by email.

Delete app account and all data

  • On the server, first: for the accounts registered on this phone, removes this phone's registration for alerts, the Pokédex lists uploaded for those accounts, and the sign-in records TwitchMiniChat keeps for them. The server decides which accounts are covered from its own record of this phone, not from what the app sends. When every step succeeds, the server keeps a deletion notice for those accounts. The notice is removed when one of those accounts signs in to TwitchMiniChat again, and otherwise kept for at least 30 days.
  • Your other devices: their registrations are not removed by this request. The next time another device signed in to one of those accounts opens the app while the notice is in place, it reads the notice and erases all of its local TwitchMiniChat data, including any other accounts on it.
  • On this phone, only after the server has confirmed: everything, including the built-in browser's website data. If the server request fails, nothing on the phone is erased and the app shows the error. You will need to sign in again.
  • Limit: the same as above. A registration left behind by an earlier "Erase everything on this device", or by a reset in a version before 5.5.1, is not reached.

Removing one account

  • On this phone: removes that account and the data kept for it, such as its alert settings, Most Wanted selection, Pokédex and inventory snapshots and catch presets. Other accounts are kept.
  • On the server: asks the server to stop alerts for that account on this phone. The account's other server data, such as uploaded Pokédex lists and sign-in records, is not deleted. The account is removed from the phone even if that request fails.

Asking for deletion by email

Email: unouidol.interactive@gmail.com
Subject: TMC Data Deletion Request

Please include:

  • Your Twitch username
  • Any relevant profile or account identifiers, if known
  • A short statement that you are requesting deletion of app-related data

An email request can cover what the in-app options cannot reach, such as a registration left behind by an earlier erase, or data for an account you no longer have on any device.

Retention exceptions

Some limited records may be retained for a short period where reasonably necessary for security, abuse prevention, fraud prevention, moderation review, audit, or legal compliance.

Response timing

Deletion requests are reviewed and processed within a reasonable timeframe.
