Data Deletion

Last updated: 2026-09-26

TwitchMiniChat has four ways to delete data from inside the app, and you can also ask for deletion by email. Three of the four contact the server. For each one, this page says what it removes from the phone and what it does, or does not do, on the server.

In the app, open Safety & Privacy, then Data & Account Control. "Reset local data" offers the first two options below; "Delete app account and all data" is a separate button. To remove a single account, use the X next to it in the account list.

Reset local data, keep accounts

  • On this phone: removes the app's settings and preferences, including alert settings, hidden users and Most Wanted selections, together with cached data, the local diagnostic record, and your acceptance of the terms, which you are asked for again. Your accounts, their sign-in with the TwitchMiniChat server, and the built-in browser's website data are kept, and so is the credential the server knows this phone by.
  • On the server: nothing. No request is sent. This phone stays registered for alerts, and the erase below can still remove that registration later.

Erase everything and remove this device from the server

  • On the server: removes this phone's registration for alerts. The data belonging to your accounts — uploaded Pokédex lists, watchlists, and the sign-in records TwitchMiniChat keeps — is not deleted, and your other devices are not affected. To delete that data, use "Delete app account and all data" instead.
  • The push notification token is deleted from this phone. Without it the phone can receive no alert at all, and the server drops this phone's registration the next time it tries to reach it. That is not the same as the record being removed the moment you tap: if nothing is ever sent to this phone again, the record can stay, with a token that no longer reaches anything.
  • On this phone: every account and its sign-in with the TwitchMiniChat server, the app's settings and preferences, hidden users, cached data, the local diagnostic record, your acceptance of the terms, and the built-in browser's website data, such as the cookies of the Pokémon Community Game pages.
  • The phone is erased even when the server cannot be reached. Erasing your own data does not depend on a working connection. If the request to remove the registration does not go through, the app tells you so and erases the phone anyway; deleting the push token is what stops the alerts. If the token deletion does not go through either, alerts can still arrive for a while, and the app keeps trying to delete the token on its own until it succeeds.
  • Clearing the built-in browser is a separate step. If its website data cannot be cleared, the erase still completes and the app says which part failed. The two are not one all-or-nothing step.
  • Limit, for registrations left by earlier versions: before 5.5.2 there was a second option, "Erase everything on this device", which erased the phone without contacting the server and left its registration in place; every reset before 5.5.1 removed the credential the server identifies this phone by, with the same result. A registration left behind either way is not reached by anything in the app. Ask for its removal by email.

Delete app account and all data

  • On the server, first: for the accounts registered on this phone, removes this phone's registration for alerts, the Pokédex lists uploaded for those accounts, and the sign-in records TwitchMiniChat keeps for them. The server decides which accounts are covered from its own record of this phone, not from what the app sends. When every step succeeds, the server keeps a deletion notice for those accounts. The notice is removed when one of those accounts signs in to TwitchMiniChat again, and otherwise kept for at least 30 days.
  • Your other devices: their registrations are not removed by this request. The next time another device signed in to one of those accounts opens the app while the notice is in place, it reads the notice and erases all of its local TwitchMiniChat data, including any other accounts on it.
  • On this phone, only after the server has confirmed: everything, including the built-in browser's website data and the push notification token. If the server request fails, nothing on the phone is erased and the app shows the error. This is the one option that stops when the server cannot be reached, because it is the only one that deletes data belonging to your accounts: erasing the phone as though it had succeeded would tell you your server-side data was gone when it was not. You will need to sign in again.
  • Limit: the same as above. A registration left behind by an "Erase everything on this device" in a version before 5.5.2, or by a reset in a version before 5.5.1, is not reached.

Removing one account

  • On this phone: removes that account and the data kept for it, such as its alert settings, Most Wanted selection, Pokédex and inventory snapshots and catch presets. Other accounts are kept.
  • On the server: asks the server to switch that account's alerts off for this phone, which makes the server drop that account from this phone's list. It does not delete that account's data: uploaded Pokédex lists, watchlists and sign-in records are kept.
  • If that request fails: the account is removed from the phone anyway, the app says that alerts for it may keep arriving for a while, and it keeps trying on its own. Until it succeeds the phone keeps that account's sign-in with the TwitchMiniChat server, because that sign-in is what lets it finish; it is removed as soon as the server confirms, and an erase removes it in any case.

Asking for deletion by email

Email: unouidol.interactive@gmail.com
Subject: TMC Data Deletion Request

Please include:

  • Your Twitch username
  • Any relevant profile or account identifiers, if known
  • A short statement that you are requesting deletion of app-related data

An email request can cover what the in-app options cannot reach, such as a registration left behind by an earlier erase, or data for an account you no longer have on any device.

Retention exceptions

Some limited records may be retained for a short period where reasonably necessary for security, abuse prevention, fraud prevention, moderation review, audit, or legal compliance.

Response timing

Deletion requests are reviewed and processed within a reasonable timeframe.
